# Theory and references

PersonLens draws on personality psychology, personality-judgment research, motivation, appraisal, and interpersonal theory to interpret behavior in context. Use this reference to look up the original papers, the concepts they introduce, and how PersonLens applies them.

## Eight perspectives

| Perspective and original source | Definition | PersonLens application | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Whole Trait Theory — [Fleeson and Jayawickreme, 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4472377/); [Fleeson, 2001](https://personality-project.org/revelle/syllabi/classreadings/fleeson.2001.pdf) | Traits have a descriptive distribution of momentary states and explanatory social-cognitive processes. | Compare conditions: someone covers urgent work but declines weekend requests. Preserve both observations before generalizing. | Selected messages cannot establish a complete state distribution or uniquely reveal its causes. |
| Realistic Accuracy Model (RAM) — [Funder, 1995](https://rap.ucr.edu/FunderPR1995.pdf) | Accurate judgment requires relevant cues to be available, detected, and appropriately used. | Link a claim to its source and context; include the refusal that qualifies an apparent pattern of agreement. | A traceable quotation or agreement among models does not establish accuracy. These stages are not a validated model score. |
| Self–Other Knowledge Asymmetry (SOKA) — [Vazire, 2010](https://www.simine.com/docs/Vazire_JPSP_2010.pdf) | Self and others have different information advantages, depending on observability and evaluativeness. | Separate calm wording from the speaker's later report of anxiety; revise the interpretation when that information arrives. | Neither the person nor the observer is always correct. Disagreement is not evidence of deficient self-knowledge. |
| Traits, adaptations, and narrative identity — [McAdams and Pals, 2006](https://www.e-stimate.dk/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/McAdams-Fundamental-Principles-for-an-Integrative-Science-of-Personality.pdf); [McAdams and McLean, 2013](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/current-directions/0963721413475622/) | Dispositions, contextual goals and adaptations, and personally constructed life stories address different aspects of a person. | Distinguish “I often help,” “I want this partnership to work,” and “I used to define myself through being needed.” | Evidence categories are application rules, not extra theoretical layers. Do not invent childhood causes or personal meaning. |
| Self-Determination Theory (SDT) — [Ryan and Deci, 2000](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf) | Autonomy, competence, and relatedness concern volition, effectiveness, and connection. | If someone welcomes responsibility but dislikes prescribed steps, discuss possible limits on autonomy, while retaining alternatives. | Autonomy is not independence. Needs are not personality types; one complaint cannot establish a motive. |
| Cognitive appraisal and coping — [Folkman et al., 1986](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3712234/); [Lazarus, 1991](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1928936/) | Emotions and coping relate to an event's personal significance and perceived coping resources. | An unanswered message may initially mean rejection to its sender; learning about illness can change that interpretation. | Distress does not establish rejection as fact. Emotion cannot uniquely identify an appraisal or cause. |
| Interpersonal circumplex and complementarity — [Sadler et al., 2009](https://uwaterloo.ca/psychology/sites/default/files/uploads/documents/Sadler_et_al_2009_JPSP_Are_We_On_the_Same_Wavelength.pdf) | Interactions vary along dominance–submission and warmth–hostility; partners' responses can coordinate probabilistically. | Examine successive proposals and concessions from both speakers, including occasions when their roles reverse. | Complementarity does not establish relationship health, compatibility, or causal blame. |
| Person-centered approach — [Rogers, 1957](https://larrygmaguire.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/The-Necessary-and-Sufficient-Conditions-of-Therapeutic-Personality-Change.pdf) | Empathic understanding, congruence, and positive regard are among proposed conditions in human therapeutic relationships. | When someone expresses envy and shame, acknowledge their experience and leave room for correction and choice. | Respecting feelings does not endorse every interpretation or action. Generated empathy does not demonstrate therapist experience or treatment effects. |

## Optional personality frameworks

The Myers–Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI) is optional, not required for PersonLens. Treat a supplied type as an attributed report; any requested type discussion remains tentative. Big Five dimensions describe openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and neuroticism. Associations between these frameworks do not authorize converting scores or validate conversational typing. For example, frequent work presentations cannot settle a person's reported preference for introversion. [McCrae and Costa, 1989](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-6494.1989.tb00759.x).

## Research resources

Use these materials when designing evaluations of evidence selection and contextual interpretation.

| Dataset and source | Possible check | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PersonalityEvd — Sun et al., 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.1115/) | Compare evidence selection and separate state/trait judgments against provided Big Five observer annotations; for example, check whether a cited utterance exists. | Dialogues concern fictional television characters; annotations use model pre-annotation and human revision. Agreement is not real-person ground truth. Filtering also limits coverage of contradictory or insufficient evidence. |
| [CaSiNo — Chawla et al., 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.254/) | Examine whether interpretation retains assigned priorities and incentives during simulated campsite negotiations; for example, requesting more water need not imply selfishness. | Real participants perform a constrained role-play. Personality comparisons require appropriate identity, scoring, and questionnaire separation; strategy labels are not personality labels. |

Choose evaluation criteria that match each source's annotations and collection setting. Review source citation, contextual explanation, and personality descriptions separately. Use the review scenarios to inspect how instructions affect responses, and consult each dataset's original publication for access and licensing.
